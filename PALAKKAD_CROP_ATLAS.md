## 🎯 UNIQUE FEATURES

### **🏆 Superlatives:**
- **Highest Rice Production**: Chittur (18,000 ha)
- **Highest Rainfall**: Mannarkad (3,500mm)
- **Lowest Rainfall (Drought-prone)**: Attappadi (1,200mm)
- **Most Diverse Crops**: Attappadi (Millets, unique dryland crops)
- **Largest Area**: Attappadi (745 sq.km)
- **Smallest Area**: Thrithala (198 sq.km)

### **🌾 Crop Specializations:**
- **Rice Belt**: Chittur, Alathur, Palakkad, Pattambi (Plains & Lowlands)
- **Spice Belt**: Kollengode, Nenmara, Mannarkad (Highlands)
- **Dryland Belt**: Attappadi only (Rainshadow zone)
- **Vegetable Hub**: Kuzhalmannam, Malampuzha
- **Coconut Zone**: Ottapalam, Pattambi (Coastal lowlands)
