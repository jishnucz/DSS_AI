🌾 Rice (നെല്ല്) - Quick Guide

Nursery 20d Transplant 15d Tillering 30d Flowering 20d Grain Fill 25d Harvest
💧 Water: Maintain 5-7cm standing water during tillering
🌱 Fertilizer: Apply Urea 30kg/ha at 3 weeks after transplanting
🐛 Watch for: Leaf blast, Brown plant hopper, Stem borer

📅 Virippu Season (June-Sep)

Ideal crops: Rice (Uma, Jyothi varieties), Vegetables
Monsoon tip: Ensure proper drainage to prevent waterlogging
Disease risk: High humidity → Watch for fungal diseases

🛡️ Emergency Pest Solutions

Leaf Blast: Tricyclazole 0.6g/L or Carbendazim 1g/L spray
BPH (Brown Hopper): Drain water, apply Imidacloprid
Stem Borer: Apply Carbofuran granules at tillering

🗓️ Palakkad Crop Calendar

Virippu (വിരിപ്പ്): June-September (Monsoon crop)
Mundakan (മുണ്ടകൻ): September-January (Second crop)
Puncha (പുഞ്ച): January-May (Summer crop, irrigated)
